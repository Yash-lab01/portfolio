💡 The Problem

Technical recruiters and talent acquisition teams drown in hundreds of applicant profiles for every open requisition. Keyword-based applicant tracking systems (ATS) frequently reject high-potential engineers due to trivial phrasing variations, while manual resume reviews introduce cognitive fatigue, subjective inconsistency, and lengthy hiring cycles.

🎯 The Solution

Recruiter AI replaces fragile keyword filters with semantic signal extraction and structured LLM evaluation. It parses complex job descriptions into Pydantic requirement schemas, extracts candidate signal vectors across technical proficiencies, seniority levels, and relevant achievements, and computes weighted match rankings with transparent score breakdowns.

⚡ Key Features

🎯

Multi-Signal Candidate Scoring

Evaluates technical skills, domain experience, education relevance, and career progression signals.

🧠

Job Description Parsing

Extracts required vs optional qualifications, seniorities, and core tech stacks into structured Pydantic schemas.

📊

Explainable Rank & Match Scores

Provides transparent percentage match scores and breakdown reasoning for why candidates were recommended.

⚡

High-Speed JSONL Batch Pipeline

Processes thousands of candidate JSONL records in minutes with lightweight CPU-optimized execution.

🛠️ Tech Stack & Technologies

LLM & Semantic Embeddings

Natural language candidate profile embedding models and zero-shot ranking prompts.

Pydantic & JSON Schema

Strict schema validation for job requirements and candidate signal extraction.

Python Data Engine

Custom scoring algorithms, NumPy matrix algebra, and JSONL batch processing.
