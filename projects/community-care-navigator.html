Community Care Navigator

Built for the Google GenAI Hackathon (Hack2Skill). An AI-powered Decision Intelligence Platform helping vulnerable populations discover welfare schemes, locate healthcare facilities, and receive personalized guidance through natural conversation in English, Hindi, and regional languages.

Google ADK Gemini 2.5 Flash Multimodal Vision BigQuery / RAG Plotly Mapbox Streamlit Python
View on GitHub →

💡 The Problem

Vulnerable citizens often miss out on life-changing government welfare programs due to language barriers, complex eligibility criteria, fragmented administrative websites, and difficulty filling out official paperwork manually.

🎯 The Solution

Community Care Navigator leverages Google ADK and Gemini 2.5 Flash to act as a warm, multilingual conversational guide. Users can speak naturally in regional languages or upload photos of official ID/income documents for instant multimodal OCR profile parsing and explainable scheme matching.

⚡ Key Features

🤝

Conversational Welfare Agent

Orchestrated with Google ADK and Gemini 2.5 Flash to provide empathetic, multilingual guidance across English, Hindi, and regional dialects.

📷

Multimodal Document Ingestion

Upload photos of Aadhaar cards, Income certificates, or Disability documents to let Gemini Vision automatically extract records and pre-fill profiles.

🔍

Explainable Scheme Matching

RAG engine matches citizens with eligible government policies and displays transparent visual checklists detailing why criteria passed.

🗺️

Interactive Healthcare Density Map

Plotly Mapbox overlays show real-time hospital locations, bed capacities, emergency contact details, and infrastructure gap analysis.

⚡

Hybrid / Local-First Database

Auto-detects GCP configurations to utilize BigQuery Native Vector Search or falls back seamlessly to local SQLite vector embeddings.

📈

Policy Analytics Dashboard

Comprehensive Streamlit UI featuring regional coverage KPIs, scheme distribution metrics, and interactive filtering.

Analytics & Maps
