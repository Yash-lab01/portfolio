Engineering Philosophy

How I Build Systems That Work in the Real World

A candid perspective on production AI: why monolithic prompts fail, where local quantization matters, and how data integrity drives every model.

PRINCIPLE 01

Determinism Over Raw Prompts

Monolithic LLM prompts hallucinate geography, fail at arithmetic, and produce erratic structures. I isolate responsibilities into stateful LangGraph nodes, enforce Pydantic contracts, and offload calculations to deterministic solvers (AST parsing, K-means++, Haversine routing) at 0 token cost.

PRINCIPLE 02

Pragmatic Latency & Cost Routing

Sending every request to a 70B cloud model is economically reckless and slow. I architect hybrid multi-tier routers: sub-millisecond TF-IDF classification, fast slot extraction via Groq, local quantized Ollama fallbacks, and frontier cloud models reserved exclusively for synthesis.

PRINCIPLE 03

Verifiable Grounding, Zero Fluff

An AI answer without verifiable source traceability is a liability. Whether building an academic paper assistant or an enterprise company scraper, every output is tethered to raw data: paragraph-level citation anchors, vector similarity scores, and strict schema validation.
